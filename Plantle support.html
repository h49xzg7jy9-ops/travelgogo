Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题

How do I use the app? 如何使用应用？ ▼

1. Add plants: Tap the "+" button, select a plant type or take a photo to identify the plant
2. Record care: Record watering, fertilizing, pruning and other care operations on the plant details page
3. View reminders: The app will intelligently remind you to care for your plants based on weather and plant characteristics
4. View history: View all historical care operations in the care records

1. 添加植物：点击"+"按钮，选择植物种类或拍照识别植物
2. 记录养护：在植物详情页面记录浇水、施肥、修剪等养护操作
3. 查看提醒：应用会根据天气和植物特性智能提醒您进行养护
4. 查看历史：在养护记录中查看所有历史养护操作

How does the app identify plants? 应用如何识别植物？ ▼

The app integrates plant identification functionality. You can identify plant species by taking photos or uploading plant photos. The identification results will provide basic information about the plant, including Chinese name, English name, scientific name, etc.

应用集成了植物识别功能，您可以通过拍照或上传植物照片来识别植物种类。识别结果会提供植物的基本信息，包括中文名、英文名、学名等。

How do care reminders work? 养护提醒是如何工作的？ ▼

The app intelligently calculates the next care time based on the characteristics of the plants you add, local weather conditions, and the time of your last care. The system combines weather factors such as temperature and humidity to provide you with personalized care suggestions and reminders.

应用会根据您添加的植物特性、当地天气情况以及您上次养护的时间，智能计算下次养护的时间。系统会结合温度、湿度等天气因素，为您提供个性化的养护建议和提醒。

Will my data sync to the cloud? 我的数据会同步到云端吗？ ▼

The app supports iCloud cloud backup. You can enable cloud backup in settings, so your plant data and care records will automatically sync to iCloud, making it easy to sync across multiple devices.

应用支持 iCloud 云备份功能。您可以在设置中开启云备份，这样您的植物数据和养护记录会自动同步到 iCloud，方便在多设备间同步。

How do I backup my plant data? 如何备份我的植物数据？ ▼

You can backup data in two ways:
1. Enable iCloud cloud backup: Enable cloud backup in settings, and data will automatically sync to iCloud
2. Export data: You can export plant data through the app's share feature and save it to other locations

您可以通过两种方式备份数据：
1. 开启 iCloud 云备份：在设置中开启云备份功能，数据会自动同步到 iCloud
2. 导出数据：您可以通过应用的分享功能导出植物数据，保存到其他位置

How do I report issues or suggest features? 如何报告问题或建议功能？ ▼

If you encounter any issues or have feature suggestions, please email jorykk11@icloud.com. We will carefully consider your feedback.

如果您遇到任何问题或有功能建议，请发送邮件至 jorykk11@icloud.com，我们会认真考虑您的反馈。
